### Cultural Advice​ 

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people should be aware that entries in the Queensland Heritage Register may contain references to deceased persons.​


​
Material drawn from historic sources may contain terms that reflect authors’ views, or those of the period in which the item was written or recorded, but which may not be considered appropriate today. While information may not reflect current understanding, it is provided in historical context.
